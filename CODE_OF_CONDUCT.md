# Code of Conduct

LiteERP contributors and participants are expected to maintain a welcoming,
professional, and harassment-free community.

Be respectful, assume good intent, accept constructive feedback, and focus on
what benefits users of the project. Harassment, discrimination, personal
attacks, and publication of private information are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and participation that
violate these expectations. Serious or repeated violations may result in a
temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Reports will
be reviewed fairly and kept confidential where reasonably possible.
